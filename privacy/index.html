First Light

PrivacyPolicy

The short version: First Light collects nothing about you, sends nothing anywhere, and has no idea who you are.

Last updated 21 September 2026

First Light has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and makes no network requests. There is no server for your data to go to.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you create in First Light is written to your iPhone and stays there:

  • Your promise and alarm settings — the time you set, what you wrote down, and the sound you chose.
  • Your voice note — the recording you make the night before, saved as an audio file inside the app's own storage.
  • Your wake history — which mornings you got up and which you didn't, so the app can show your routine.
  • Your preferences — the handful of settings you've changed.

None of it is transmitted, backed up to me, or shared with anyone. I have no ability to read it.

Microphone

First Light asks for microphone access for exactly one purpose: recording the short voice note that plays back when your alarm rings. Recording happens only while you are deliberately recording, and the audio is saved to your device and never uploaded. You can delete a recording at any time from within the app, and re-record whenever you like.

Notifications

Your alarm is scheduled locally by iOS on your own device. First Light sends no push notifications, so there is no remote server that knows your alarm time.

iCloud and backups

First Light does not sync to iCloud. If you have iPhone backups switched on in iOS, your app data may be included in your own encrypted backup — that is between you and Apple, under Apple's terms, and I have no access to it.

Analytics and tracking

There are none. First Light does not track you across apps or websites, does not use an advertising identifier, and contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Apple provides anonymous download and crash figures to me through App Store Connect; those are aggregated by Apple, are not linked to you, and never pass through the app.

Deleting your data

Deleting a promise, a recording or a history entry in the app removes it from your device. Deleting First Light removes all of it at once. Because nothing was ever sent anywhere, there is nothing left for me to hold and nothing you need to ask us to erase.

Children

First Light is not directed at children under 13, and collects no personal information from anyone of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
