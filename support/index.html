My alarm didn't ring

Almost always one of these, in order of likelihood:

  • The silent switch. The physical switch on the side of your iPhone (or the Action Button set to Silent) mutes notification sounds, and an alarm from an app like First Light is a notification. Your iPhone's built-in Clock app is the one exception Apple allows. Keep your phone on ring overnight.
  • Ringer volume. Turn it up before bed — the volume buttons change media volume while something is playing, which is not the same setting.
  • A Focus that blocks notifications. If you use Sleep Focus or Do Not Disturb, allow First Light through it. The app's intro walks you through this — see "Replay the intro" below.
  • Notifications switched off. Settings → First Light → Notifications → Allow Notifications, with Sounds on.
  • Low Power Mode. It can delay background activity on some iPhones.

Can I see the setup instructions again?

Yes — open Settings inside the app and tap Replay the intro. It walks through notifications, ringer and Focus again. Your promises and history are kept; replaying it changes nothing else.

Why can I only set one alarm?

That's deliberate. Stacking six alarms trains you to ignore six of them. First Light gives you one, tied to one reason you wrote down the night before. Setting a new promise retires the old one.

How do I change or delete my voice note?

Open the promise you're editing and record again — the new recording replaces the old one. You can also delete it outright, and the alarm will still ring normally without it.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Completely. First Light never goes online. Aeroplane mode makes no difference to your alarm, as long as your phone is on and not silenced.

What happens to my data?

It stays on your iPhone. Nothing is collected, nothing is uploaded, there is no account. Full detail on the privacy page.
